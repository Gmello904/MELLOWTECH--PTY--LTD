Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. This page explains what the MellowTech Security Platform (the “platform”) uses and why. It forms part of our Privacy Policy.

1.What we use

NamePurposeTypeLasts
sessionKeeps you signed in and protects your account against forged requests.Essential, set by usUp to 8 hours

Both items are essential. The platform cannot keep you signed in or protect your account without them.

2.Third parties

  • PayPal and Payfast: when you open Billing & Plans or pay, these companies may set their own cookies. Their policies explain how they use them.
  • Google and GitHub: if you choose to sign in with them, they may set cookies on their own pages.

3.What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track you across other websites. We do not use analytics cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

4.Your choices

You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of the platform and of this page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but the platform will not work without the essential session cookie.

5.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version and effective date at the top show when it last changed.
