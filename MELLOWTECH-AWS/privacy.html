1.Who is responsible

MellowTech Group (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2025/457277/07, Pretoria, South Africa, is the responsible party for the personal information described here.

Our Information Officer is Given Mpho Mello. Privacy questions and requests go to mphogivenmello@gmail.com.

2.What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, company name and role. Your password is stored only as a scrambled hash, never as readable text.
  • Sign-in with Google or GitHub: your name and verified email address, if you choose that way to sign in.
  • Devices you connect: device name, operating system, IP address, processor, memory and disk use, security status (such as antivirus, firewall, disk encryption and updates), security alerts, and the actions you send to the device.
  • Security information you give us: events and alerts, blocked addresses, domains you add for scanning and the results, and text or files you paste into our tools (for example logs or cloud settings).
  • Questions you ask Ask Mellow: your question and the incident and device information needed to answer it.
  • Documents you scan: the file name, type, a fingerprint (hash) of the file and the scan result. We do not keep the document itself.
  • Payments: PayPal and Payfast handle your card details. We keep only your plan, the amount, the status and the order reference.
  • Technical and activity records: your IP address and a log of important actions on your account (for example sign-ins and changes), kept for security.

3.Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for, including security monitoring, alerts and the automatic actions you switch on.
  • To create and manage your account, approve new organisations and take payment.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent abuse and investigate problems.
  • To send service emails, such as confirmation and password reset messages.
  • To meet legal duties.

We rely on your consent, on our contract with you, on our legitimate interest in running a secure service, and on legal obligations.

4.Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the platform, and only as far as they need it:

  • Hosting: Amazon Web Services, which hosts the platform.
  • Email: Google (Gmail), which delivers our service emails such as confirmation and password reset messages.
  • Payments: PayPal and Payfast, which process payments.
  • Sign-in: Google or GitHub, if you use them to sign in.
  • Ask Mellow: Groq, the artificial intelligence provider that writes answers to the questions you ask. Your question and the incident and device details needed to answer it are sent to it.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

5.Information outside South Africa

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. Where this happens we make sure the provider is bound by law, a binding agreement or similar protections that give your information a level of protection comparable to POPIA, as POPIA requires.

6.How long we keep it

Account informationWhile your account is active, and for 12 months after it closes.
Security records and logs12 months.
Payment records5 years, as tax and accounting law requires.

After these periods we delete the information or make it anonymous, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7.How we protect it

We store passwords as hashes, keep each organisation’s data separate, record important actions in an audit log, limit repeated sign-in attempts and protect forms against forged requests. The platform is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

No system is perfectly secure. If personal information is compromised, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected, as the law requires.

8.Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw your consent. Email mphogivenmello@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
